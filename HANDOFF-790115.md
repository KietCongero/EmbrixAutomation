4. RESUME order ORD-40096/40099 duplicates were created by payment retries (cancelled later with the account).

7. Collections page: units of paid invoices stay listed as CLOSED with their last planned action (e.g. 418817's INACTIVATE_SUBSCRIPTION 03-21 after the grace); any row lookup by date alone picks them up.
